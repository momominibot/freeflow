Third-party pictures in this folder
===================================

Most of the pictures here are shapes from Simple Icons (CC0 1.0, https://simpleicons.org), drawn in each brand's own
colour by scripts/art/make_app_logos.sh. CC0 asks for nothing; they are listed so it is clear where they came from.
The names and marks belong to their owners. Purrscribe shows them only to say which app a tile is for.
